What Tour Planner Does

Three core capabilities, built for travelers who want to spend less time searching and more time planning.

Flight Search

Search round trips from Seoul (Incheon/Gimpo) to major Japanese airports. Filter by departure time window. Results are sorted by total cost including airport transfer estimates.

Prices shown are mockup examples for demonstration.

Hotel Estimate

Adds an estimated nightly hotel rate to each trip and links to hotel search sites for the same dates.

Sample rates only — not live inventory.

Price Compare

Ranks every possible date in your travel month by total trip cost and shows the top three with a full line-item breakdown.

All figures are illustrative until live API integration.

How It Works

Four steps from idea to itinerary.

1

Set Your Trip

Choose origin, Japanese arrival airport, travel month, and trip length. Set your departure time window and airport transport.

2

Search Options

The tool checks every start date in the month that fits your trip length and time window.

3

Compare & Rank

Results are ranked by total cost. You see the top options with a full breakdown of each line item.

4

Book Externally

Click through to the provider to complete your booking. Tour Planner does not process payments.
